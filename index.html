Guide

What is the Composite Index?

It's a single number that tells you, at a glance, whether produce is generally more expensive or cheaper right now than it was over the past week. 100 means "about the same as last week's average." Above 100 means prices have risen overall; below 100 means they've eased.

What do the badges mean?

  • Value Buy — this item is noticeably cheaper than its 7-day average. Good time to stock up.
  • Normal — price is close to its usual range.
  • Pricey — this item costs more than usual right now. You might wait a few days if you can.

Top Gainers & Best Deals

Gainers are items whose prices climbed the most; Best Deals are items whose prices dropped the most. Use the 7-day / last month / last year tabs to change what the comparison is measured against.

Compare tab

Pick any item to see how its price varies by state, district, city, and individual mandi on the same day — useful for spotting whether your local mandi is charging more than nearby markets.

Thali Cost Index

An estimate of what it costs today to put together one standard home-style meal (roti or rice, a vegetable, and dal), using the same modal mandi prices shown elsewhere in the app.

Shopping List

Tap "+" on any item to add it to your basket. Adjust quantity in the basket drawer, tick items off while you shop, and share the list on WhatsApp or copy it as plain text before you leave.

Where does the data come from?

Prices are sourced from Agmarknet (Government of India) via data.gov.in, refreshed daily between 7–8 AM IST. If the day's data isn't published yet, the app shows the most recent day available.
